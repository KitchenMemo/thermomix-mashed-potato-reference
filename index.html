Quick reference

Key rules
  • Floury potatoes (Russet, Maris Piper, King Edward, Royal Blue) give lighter, fluffier mash; yellow all-purpose potatoes (Yukon Gold) give richer, creamier mash.
  • Weigh the potatoes and cut them into even pieces so they finish cooking together.
  • Cook until completely tender, then finish briefly. Do not extend the mixing time to chase a perfectly smooth look.
  • Butterfly whisk: never above speed 4 (TM6 manual), and attach it correctly before starting.
  • Gluey mash cannot reliably be restored. Repurpose it as a baked potato dish.
